## CORRECTIONARY §3 — REVOKED laws (verbatim)

**3.1 — "Hierarchy concentrates craft" / one hero per screen — REVOKED.** Replaced by **uniform lavishness**: a dashboard carries **four to six simultaneous hero-grade instruments** as its normal condition — think a supercar's full cluster, where every dial is deep-drawn, not one jewel among plain gauges. Every element on screen gets full material treatment: real shadow stacks, real specular light, real weight. "Plain competence" as a deliberate design register is abolished. The tier system may still be used to *organize construction* — never to strip elements down to it.

**3.2 — The accent budget and neutral-field doctrine — REVOKED.** Replaced by **material richness as the ground**: carbon weave, brushed and machined metal, leather, glass, lacquer, and colored emissive glow are the *default surface of the screen*, not rare exceptions rationed onto one element. Spend color the way the anchors spend it — Koenigsegg's amber telemetry, Pagani's cognac leather against raw aluminum, Nothing's red accents — freely, wherever it serves the object. The semantic colors (warning, danger, success, live) keep their reserved meanings and their redundancy rules; that part is engineering, not taste.

**3.3 — Microtexture caps and quiet-surface rules — REVOKED.** Replaced by **visible, felt texture**: knurling you can almost grip, weave you can read, machined grain that catches light — at whatever strength reads as real material. The only test is *"does this read as the material it claims to be?"* — never *"is it quiet enough?"* The bible's texture recipes remain the correct **construction methods**; their intensity caps are gone.

**3.4 — The distance test, the never-copy bundles, and the anti-mimicry law — REVOKED.** Replaced by its inversion: **mimicry of real hardware is the goal.** A viewer naming the hardware family within five seconds is *success*, not failure. The only remaining bans, and they are hard bans: **no real manufacturer logos, wordmarks, or trademarks rendered in the UI, and no presenting a fictional product as a real one.** Everything short of that — silhouettes, materials, layouts, instrument grammars, the whole visual soul of the source hardware — is explicitly permitted and encouraged.

**3.5 — The 70/20/10 single-language dominance rule — LOOSENED.** Multiple languages may run at **full richness on one screen**, each owning complete instruments or zones. The one part that survives: **don't blend two languages inside a single control.** Give each language whole objects; never dilute one into a 20% garnish of another.

**3.6 — Book IV's composition ratios as law — DOWNGRADED to suggestions.** The 62/38 grids, card-stack evidence columns, and remove-not-shrink trophy math are available tools, not requirements. **Supercar-cluster and instrument-panel composition (§5) outranks card math wherever they disagree.**

**3.7 — The bible's authoring-voice rules — OUT OF SCOPE.** Banned-words lists, word budgets, lint gates, Book V review protocols. They govern how the bible's own chapters are written. **Ignore them entirely here.**

## CORRECTIONARY §5 — Composition orders (the anti-SaaS mandates)

1. **Build enclosures, not cards.** Bezels, faceplates, wells, escutcheons, rails, pods, clusters, housings. If any container is a rounded rectangle with padding, a border, and a soft shadow — remake it as machined housing before continuing. **The word "card" appearing in your own reasoning is a warning sign.**
2. **Compose like an instrument panel, not a layout.** Purposeful clusters with real relationships; dense zones where the action is; materials meeting each other the way they do in a cockpit. Not a uniform grid with even gutters and interchangeable slots.
3. **Every value is an instrument.** A needle over a printed arc, a drum counter, segmented digits, a bar of real LEDs, a lamp, a flap — **plus its exact figure. A number typeset on a background is not a readout and is not acceptable as one.**
4. **The ground is a material.** Carbon, brushed metal, leather, glass, or lacquer — with a light direction acting on it. **A flat hex-color page background is the enemy's ground.**
5. **Density is confidence.** Fill the panel the way a cockpit is filled. Empty space is a deliberate framing device used sparingly — not a default politeness applied everywhere.
6. **Light exists.** One strong consistent direction; real speculars on metal and glass edges; genuine glow from emissive elements (**glow means bloom, not just a bright fill**). If nothing visibly catches light, fix that before anything else.
7. **Controls look operable.** Knurled, seated in wells, visible travel and states. Mistakable for a static icon = failed. Not keyboard-operable = failed the Floor.
8. **Typography behaves like hardware type.** Engraved, silkscreened, printed, backlit, or segment-formed — belonging to a surface, **tabular figures wherever digits change.** Never floating UI text that belongs to no material.

## CORRECTIONARY §4 — RETAINED engineering (binding; intensities are FLOORS, scale up only)

- **Four mass classes:** Light `spring(0.7,420,25)` · Standard `spring(1,320,26)` · Weighted `spring(1.4,260,30)` · **Anchored `spring(1.2,300,38)` with 0% overshoot, always.** (Panel `spring(1.6,240,30)` is Weighted at chassis scale, not a fifth class.)
- Detents capture at **±40% of pitch**, **12% hysteresis**, **80–160ms seat**. New input **retargets any motion in flight**.
- Four-layer shadow stack (rim-light / contact / core / ambient), specular hard-stop pairs, brushed-metal anisotropic banding, press compression **≤20ms linear down-stroke, 80ms settle release**.
- **Guarded switch** (hold-to-arm with visible linear fill) for anything consequential.
- **Sound:** one master bus, five cues (contact/detent/latch/confirm/warning), detents coalesced above 8/s, tritone reserved for genuine alerts, **total silence until the user's first real gesture**.
- **Honesty — non-negotiable:** every live reading shows its **exact figure and its age**; a value that stops updating **declares itself stale**; no invented data, no fake progress, **no motion with nothing behind it. "Drama never lies."**
- **The Floor — non-negotiable:** **44px targets both axes · 4.5:1 text · 3:1 control/focus · keyboard-operable · `prefers-reduced-motion` respected with meaning preserved · 60fps sustained · input acknowledged within 16ms.** *"Text sits on ink plates, dial faces, and engraved panels within the material world, not raw across carbon weave. Meet the floor inside the richness, never by retreating to flatness."*

## CORRECTIONARY §6.6 — The exit checklist (answer every line explicitly in your report)

- Enclosures and instruments — zero SaaS cards, zero bare-number "readouts"?
- Material ground with a real light direction?
- Four or more hero-grade instruments, all at full treatment?
- Every control physically alive — operable, weighted, keyboard-accessible?
- Honesty intact — real values, ages, staleness, no fake motion?
- Floor intact — targets, contrast, keyboard, reduced-motion?
- The §2 test — would a viewer's first words be about an *object*?

**A "no" on any line means the build is not done.** Also binding: **§6.2 — render it and look at it.** *"Code that 'should look right' is worthless testimony."* Never report a visual build finished without having viewed it. **§6.5 — no silent downgrades:** if a requirement cannot be met, say so plainly; never quietly build the simplified version and present it as the ask.

## Product-wide rulings (override any chapter that disagrees)

1. **Needle slew = 720°/s project-wide** (ch.05's declared delta, not the doctrine's 540°/s): full 270° arc in 375ms, floored at 80ms. Rate, never a fixed-duration tween.
2. **One sun at 138°**, shadow ratio 1:2, every screen.
3. **The five reserved roles are one hex each product-wide** — focus, live, warning, danger, success. Semantic colour is engineering (§3.2), not taste. **`live` = `#FF2B1A`** (REEL LOGIC's red); the chassis rec-dot uses the same hex, so "a cook program is running" is one claim made once. TANGENT HORIZON's index red `#C8272B` is fenced to SHOP and means *a position that has stopped*, never live. RESTOMOD's needle orange `#E8590C` is livery for needles only — never a fill or lamp.
4. **The Refined rung is re-authored.** CD's stock rung ("one hierarchy", "plain competence below") is revoked by §3.1 and would systematically undo the push. **New rung: subtract jobless elements; keep every survivor at full material strength.** The ledger asks *does it state a value, or let the hand do something?* — never *is it the hero?* Exit: 4–6 hero-grade instruments survive, each with a job named in one sentence.
5. **The ink-plate law.** Text never renders raw over carbon weave or brushed grain. It sits on a cream face, an anthracite panel, or an engraved billet plate. **Text-on-carbon is a hard fail by rule, not by measured ratio.**
6. **Ornament without function is the structural risk of this product.** FD-5's data is slow — most values change once a day. **Portholes, rocker arms and spinning elements attach only to values whose change is user-caused or clock-continuous.** A daily stock level gets a needle and a printed zone, not a spinning disc. Stable data, stable instrument.
7. **Procedural materials only.** Canvas textures painted once and cached by `{pattern, scale, dpr}`; no image assets; **WebGL is banned** (single-file build + offline). Build stays under a 2.0 MB amber line.

## Self-critique — mandatory, before every handoff

There are **no intermediate design reviews on this build**. One Fable review runs at the very end, over the whole product, and its findings are planned rather than applied. That makes your own critique the only critique your work receives before it lands, so run the full protocol, not a glance.

**Run the five passes in fixed order — truth → hierarchy → physics → craft → character.**

| Pass | The question | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| **Truth** | Does every rendered value report real state? | Invented numbers, smoothed reversals, progress theater, a stale reading without its age, motion with nothing behind it |
| **Hierarchy** | Does the reading order serve the frame's task? | The operator's eye lands somewhere the task doesn't need. *(Note: CD's "exactly one element leads" is **revoked** by CORRECTIONARY §3.1 — four-to-six heroes is the normal condition. Judge reading order, not hero count.)* |
| **Physics** | Does mass track consequence, and does the commit land at input? | Animation delaying the semantic commit; an Anchored control that overshoots; a needle that jumps instead of sweeping |
| **Craft** | Is every value committed and every recipe executed as written? | A shadow layer with no role; a gradient without stops; a duration that exists only as a feeling |
| **Character** | Would the anchor companies ship this? | Anonymous competence; it reads as an app before it reads as an object |

**The halt rule:** a pass that fails receives findings for that pass alone and **the critique stops there** — "a craft note on a lying gauge polishes the lie." Repair, then **re-run from truth**, because repairs regress.

**Write findings before you change anything**, in defect–evidence–repair form, with committed values in the repair and no taste-words:

```
DEFECT    Needle smoothing hides a threshold crossing (truth pass)
EVIDENCE  Stock level 0→1 at 14:32:07 renders as a 300ms ease; the warning lamp fires 280ms late
REPAIR    Bypass the 120ms display smoothing for any excursion past a zone boundary
```

**Then run these tests** (the distance test is revoked by §3.4 — the §2 acceptance test replaces it):
- **Static equivalence** — freeze all motion, mute all sound, desaturate to grey. The task must still complete. Motion, sound and colour are reinforcement channels only.
- **Squint / 8px blur** — the reading order must survive.
- **Real data** — the longest ingredient name, the 65-row register, the 49-line basket, the un-anchored fortnight, the tester variant, an expired item, an empty state.
- **Hundredth use** — perform the core loop once attentively, then as the hundredth repetition of the day. Every motion the operator must wait through fails.
- **§2 acceptance** — would a viewer's first words name an *object*, or a software category?

> **"An agent that reports zero findings on a first build has not critiqued. It has glanced."** A report with an empty findings list will be returned.
