FD-5 · MEAL — the escalation ladder

16 FACETED VOLUME, on the tray that answers what exactly goes on this plate, at what portion, and can it be cooked right now? Every rung below runs. Real data throughout, read off the running app on 2026-08-18 — see the source comment for the full set.

Rung 1 · Simple

Grayscale ramp only — #141414 to #F2F2F2. No gradient, no shadow stack, no bloom, no texture, no spring, no sound. Values snap frame to frame. The state selector below really drives it: all nine states, not nine drawings of states.

Verified on this rung: the plate's name and slot, the portion figure, the per-ingredient grams, the total weight, the coverage percentage and its age. The task completes — read the plate, set the portion with the − and + keys, read whether it can be cooked. Nothing below this rung adds a fact; everything below adds a body.

Rung 2 · Pushed

Four studies, one axis each. Everything not on a study's axis stays at the Simple rung, so each claim is testable on its own.

Study A — the detent (physics)

Claim. Thirteen seats at a 22.5° pitch, seating in 110ms on cubic-bezier(.60,0,.10,1), with a 12% hysteresis band and NO coast at any speed, reports a portion the hand committed to. A free rotary with inertia reports a portion the hand merely swept past. Drag it fast and release: the value stops where you let go.

Against Simple: Simple's − and + move the same value and land in the same places, so the VALUE is identical — which is the point. What this study adds is that the control now costs something to move and cannot be moved by accident, and that a fast throw and a slow one commit exactly the same number of decisions.

Study B — the finish (material)

Claim. Four surfaces keyed to ch.16 section 2 — gunmetal field under one 138° sun with a hard-edged facet catch, the recessed well, hard-mirror chrome cut into sixteen planes, and the black-edition drum face — read as cut metal rather than as four greys. The mirror's two stops are this world's own darkened pair, #9FA4A7 to #EDEFEF.

Against Simple: Simple's #1f1f1f and #2c2c2c are two values of one nothing. These four are a metal, a cut into it, a polished part turning in it, and the drum face behind them — and the mirror is the only one that changes when it turns, because the catch stays fixed to the sun (II.2.4).

Study C — the ring (the licensed emissive)

Claim. One ring, two arcs of 166° with 2° gaps and 24° reserved at the base, bound to WHICH COVER the plate is cut for — a categorical fact — and never to the portion. Press a cover key: the lit arc moves, the dim print holds its place, the lens rims stay in both states, and the word changes with the light.

Against Simple: Simple prints "her · 70 kg" as a word and nothing else. The ring adds a second channel that survives a squint and an 8px blur — and it adds no third channel, because the word never leaves.

Study D — the porthole (mechanism)

Claim. A geared disc at 2:1 — 45° of disc per 22.5° of wheel, one tooth per committed seat — lets the steps a portion has taken be counted off the mechanism instead of inferred from the figure. Its cumulative turn lives in dataset, because a computed matrix wraps at ±180° and this disc crosses that after four seats.

Against Simple: Simple's figure changes and nothing else does, so a click and a no-op look identical. Here the mechanism moves one tooth per decision, and the telemetry shows the dataset turn passing 180° and 360° while the matrix read stays inside ±180° — which is the whole reason the count is not recovered from the matrix.

Rung 4 · Signature

The one move only this language would make. ch.16 section 7: "Signature retires the flat conic disc and cuts the wheel's own circumference into individual facet planes via a layered ring geometry, so the one control's edge becomes the palette's chamfer made physical rather than merely adjacent to it."

Built with the facet count DERIVED rather than borrowed: sixteen planes at a 22.5° pitch, which is the detent pitch itself, so exactly one plane passes the fixed catch per committed seat. The mirror flash is the step. 360 ÷ 22.5 = 16.

What this rung is not allowed to do

The facet ring turns only while the value turns. There is no idle spin, no coast at any traversal length, and no free-scrub state anywhere beneath it — release mid-throw and the value stops where the hand left it, because a coast is a decision the hand never confirmed. The catch never turns with the ring: it stays fixed to the 138° sun, which is II.2.4's split and, in the chapter's own words, what "carries most of the illusion of a real part." And the ring beside it reports the cover, never the number the wheel is setting.
