2 · Pushed — three studies, one exaggerated axis each

3 · Refined — subtract jobless elements, keep every survivor at full strength

CD's stock Refined rung ("one hierarchy; plain competence below") is revoked by CORRECTIONARY §3.1 and re-authored by CD-BRIEF ruling 4. This rung subtracts elements that do no job. The chassis's hero instrument is also its only control, and its freshness is read in the same light the report is made from. And when the reading goes stale the field does not print a second fact about it; its lit cells decay toward the dim floor, so staleness is read in the light the pattern is already made of.
