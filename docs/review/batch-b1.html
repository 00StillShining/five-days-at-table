Breakfast burrito b-d1b fresh

Monday Breakfast · 9 min · origin: Tex-Mex

rev B · LINT: PASS (0 findings)

old (Phase 0, data/raw/methods.json)

  1. Black beans (60/80 g) into a small pan with a splash of water, a pinch of cumin and salt. Low heat, 3 minutes, mashing about a third of them against the side.
  2. Non-stick pan, dry, medium. Spinach (60/80 g) for 60 seconds to wilt. Out, and squeezed.
  3. Eggs (100/150 g — 2 / 3) beaten with salt. Into the pan on medium-LOW, stirred slowly and constantly. Off the heat while they still look visibly wet.
  4. Tortilla (45/68 g) warmed 20 seconds a side. His portion is a tortilla and a half — warm both.
  5. A line down the middle: beans, egg, cheddar (12/18 g), salsa (40/50 g), spinach, avocado (30/45 g).
  6. Fold the sides in, roll tight, then 60 seconds seam-side down in the dry pan to seal it shut.

why: Take the eggs off wet. They keep cooking inside a rolled burrito for a good minute afterwards, and eggs that looked perfect in the pan will be rubber by the time you unwrap it.

Chicken parmigiana with courgette b-d1d fresh

Monday Dinner · 28 min · origin: Italian-American

rev B · LINT: PASS (0 findings)

old (Phase 0, data/raw/methods.json)

  1. Oven to 200 °C fan.
  2. Sauce: onion (30/40 g) diced into 3 g of the oil, 4 minutes. Passata (100/120 g), garlic, oregano and a pinch of sugar. Simmer 10 minutes while you do everything else.
  3. Chicken (90/120 g) butterflied, opened out flat, then bashed to 1 cm. Salt both sides.
  4. Panko (18/22 g) mixed with the parmesan (12/15 g) and the rest of the oil, rubbed through with your fingers until it looks like damp sand. No egg wash — press it onto the damp chicken dry and firm.
  5. Courgette (180/200 g) in 1 cm coins, tossed with salt, spread on the tray around the chicken.
  6. 18 minutes at 200 °C. Then mozzarella (35/45 g) torn over the chicken only, 5 minutes more until it blisters.
  7. Sauce spooned around the base, never over the crumb.

why: Parmesan in the crumb instead of egg beneath it. An egg wash makes a thick soft layer that steams under the cheese; a dry parmesan-and-oil crumb pressed onto damp chicken sticks well enough and stays crisp.

  1. Fish (160/210 g) dried hard, salted, dusted with a little all-purpose seasoning.
