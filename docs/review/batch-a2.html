Thursday Breakfast · 10 min · origin: New York

rev B · LINT: PASS (0 findings)

old (Phase 0, data/raw/methods.json)

  1. Steak (55/58 g) out of the fridge 10 minutes ahead. Dry it hard with kitchen paper. Salt heavily.
  2. Pan on high until it faintly smokes. 3 g oil. Steak in — 90 seconds, flip, 90 seconds. Onto a board to rest. This is not optional.
  3. Bread (45/50 g) toasting while it rests.
  4. Same pan, heat down to medium, 3 g oil. Onion (20/25 g) 1 minute, spinach (60/80 g) in to wilt, out.
  5. Remaining oil. Eggs (50/75 g — 1 / 1½) cracked in, lid on, 2 minutes for a set white and a soft yolk.
  6. Spinach onto the toast, sliced tomato (60/80 g) salted, steak sliced AGAINST the grain, egg on top. The pan juices spooned over — that is what the avocado was covering for.

why: Dry meat, smoking pan. Steak this thin has about three minutes before it goes grey all the way through — the crust has to form fast, and it cannot form on a wet surface.

Turkey bolognese with edamame spaghetti a-d4l batch

Thursday Lunch · 8 min · origin: Bologna, reconsidered

rev B · LINT: PASS (0 findings)

old (Phase 0, data/raw/methods.json)

  1. Big pan of water on, salted hard.
  2. Sunday's bolognese into a pan on medium with a splash of water, 5 minutes.
  3. Edamame spaghetti (34/38 g) into the boiling water. 6–7 minutes, no more — it goes from firm to mush quickly and there is no rescuing it.
  4. Drain, keeping a mug of the cooking water. Pasta INTO the sauce, not sauce onto pasta.
  5. Toss hard for 30 seconds with a splash of that water and the oil (13/21 g), until it turns glossy and clings.
  6. Parmesan (10/14 g) off the heat.

why: Edamame pasta has no gluten and almost no starch, so it will not emulsify a sauce the way wheat does — the oil and the splash of cooking water are doing that job by force. Skip them and the sauce just sits in a puddle underneath.
