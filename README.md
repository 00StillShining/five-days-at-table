# FD-5 — Five Days at Table

A two-week rotating meal plan for two people training four days a week, built for fat
loss with muscle retention, and costed against real UK supermarket prices.

## The shop

Three-week cycle. **£257 per shop, about £316/month, £5.21 per person per day.**

- **Morrisons ~£145** — fresh produce, dairy, frozen, cupboard. Cheapest on almost every
  fresh line. Its Fat Free Greek Style Yoghurt 1 kg at £2.10 is the anchor.
- **Sainsbury's ~£103** — protein, eggs, and the two specialist items:
  **Mission Carb Balance wraps** (£1.60 with Nectar, a third of the Ocado equivalent) and
  **Sainsbury's Red Lentil Penne** (£1.90). Chicken breast 2 kg £12.29, Aldi Price Matched.
  **Allinson's Soya & Linseed loaf** £1.50 replaces the specialist low-carb bread.
- **Lewisham market / Caribbean grocer ~£9.50** — plantain and egusi. No supermarket stocks them.

Ocado was dropped: 25–30% more expensive on like-for-like once Morrisons matched the yoghurt.

## Decisions worth remembering

- **Whey was cut.** It supplied 0.74 g of protein per person per day. Extra skyr replaces it.
- **Tropical fruit was cut.** Mango and pineapple cost £29/month and supplied 52 kcal and
  0.6 g protein per person per day. Banana and apple do the same job for a sixth of the price.
  Pineapple survives only in the jerk salmon slaw, tinned in juice.
- **Rice your own cauliflower.** £1.19 a head against £4.92/kg pre-riced.
- **Go frozen where the dish is cooked anyway** — spinach, okra, prawns.
- **Three weeks is the sweet spot.** Four weeks saves 15p per person per day and needs
  another 11 kg of freezer space.
- **No peas anywhere.** Rice and peas uses red kidney beans; the Nigerian bean dishes are off.

## Caveats

Macros are estimates from standard composition values and drift a few percent by brand.
Meat and fish are raw weights, grains and pasta dry, tinned pulses drained.
Roughly 43% of prices were read live from the retailers' sites; the rest are estimates,
flagged in orange in the tool. Morrisons is the least verified and the largest share of spend.

General guidance on eating, not medical or dietary advice.
