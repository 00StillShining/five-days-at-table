One run of Week A's recipe quantities — but the day-7 top-up items (marked orange) are bought double, up front, so there's no second trip. That's built for slower eating: 1-2 recipe-meals a day rather than 3 means this stretches to roughly two weeks. Two items are the honest weak point even doubled — salad tomatoes and little gem lettuce don't keep 14 days no matter how you store them, so expect the last few days of each to be past their best. Everything else on this list genuinely holds. Tap a line to tick it off.
