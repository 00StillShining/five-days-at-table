REV A · FOR REVIEW

You asked whether a two-week shop is possible. It is — but only with six items switched to frozen, a disciplined freezer, and one small top-up on day 7. Six things in this basket genuinely cannot survive fourteen days, and no amount of planning changes that. The good news is what they cost: £10.69.

£183.06 Day 0 · the big shop

54 lines across three shops. Includes the whole cupboard, which then lasts well beyond this fortnight.

£10.69 Day 7 · the top-up

Six lines. Ten minutes. The only things that genuinely will not keep for two weeks.

12.6kg Into the freezer on day 0

Roughly 14 litres — a standard fridge-freezer drawer and a half. This is the real constraint, not the fridge.

£292/mo Steady state

The first fortnight is £194 because you're stocking a cupboard. Repeat fortnights settle at £136.

Three cuts I proposed that died at the shelf

the price check killed them — here is why
Downgrade the eggsMorrisons large free-range 12-pack is £3.30 against Sainsbury's £3.35. There is no cheaper free-range tier — the only saving left is barn eggs, worth about £4/month, and that's your call rather than a modelling decision.
10% beef mince instead of 5%Morrisons 10% mince is £11.20/kg. Sainsbury's 5% in a 1 kg pack is £8.50/kg. The leaner mince is the cheaper mince — buy 5% and add a little oil to the pan when you smash it.
Frozen cauliflower riceReady-riced is £4.83/kg. But frozen FLORETS are £1.50/kg — cheaper than whole heads — so the lever survives in a better form: buy frozen florets and blitz them yourself.

Day-7 top-up6 items

Cottage cheese, natural2 weeks sealed · 5 days openFridgeThe shortest-dated thing in the chiller. One 300 g tub on day 0, one on day 7 — buying both up front is the single most likely thing to go off on you.
Tomatoes, ripe7–10 daysCounter, not fridge2.4 kg, and the largest single top-up line. Keep them out of the fridge — cold kills the flavour and doesn't extend the life much.
Little gem or romaine7–10 daysFridgeWhole heads, not bagged leaves. Bagged salad is four days; a whole gem is ten.
Pineapple4 days cutFridgeFrozen would work but costs more here, and Week B's big 460 g use falls on day 9 anyway. One pack on day 0, the rest on day 7.

Freeze on day 010 items

Chicken breast, skinless2 days fresh · 6 months frozenFreezer → fridgeSplit at the till: 770 g stays out for Week A's Sunday roast, 1,270 g goes straight to the freezer in one bag. Down to the fridge on the Friday night before Week B.

Mozzarella, reduced fat, block3 weeks sealed · 4 days openFreezer → fridgeOne 250 g block, opened twice eight days apart — it will not survive that. Cut it in half on day 0 and freeze one half. Mozzarella freezes badly for salad and perfectly for melting, which is all we do with it.

Tortilla, high-protein low-carb3 weeks sealed · 1 week openFreezerThree packs, and only one gets opened at a time. Freeze two on day 0; they thaw in fifteen minutes on the counter.
