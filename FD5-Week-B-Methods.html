Breakfast burrito

Ingredients
IngredientHerHim

  1. Black beans (60/80 g) into a small pan with a splash of water, a pinch of cumin and salt. Low heat, 3 minutes, mashing about a third of them against the side.

  2. Non-stick pan, dry, medium. Spinach (60/80 g) for 60 seconds to wilt. Out, and squeezed.

  3. Eggs (100/150 g — 2 / 3) beaten with salt. Into the pan on medium-LOW, stirred slowly and constantly. Off the heat while they still look visibly wet.

  4. Tortilla (45/68 g) warmed 20 seconds a side. His portion is a tortilla and a half — warm both.

  5. A line down the middle: beans, egg, cheddar (12/18 g), salsa (40/50 g), spinach, avocado (30/45 g).

  6. Fold the sides in, roll tight, then 60 seconds seam-side down in the dry pan to seal it shut.

WhyTake the eggs off wet. They keep cooking inside a rolled burrito for a good minute afterwards, and eggs that looked perfect in the pan will be rubber by the time you unwrap it.

  1. Rice (45/60 g dry) on, 25 minutes covered. This one is cooked fresh — it is the end of the week and Sunday's is long gone.

  2. Teriyaki: 3 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp mirin or rice vinegar, 1 tsp honey, grated ginger and garlic. Into a small pan, simmered 3–4 minutes until it just coats a spoon.

  3. Salmon (110/145 g) dried, into a hot pan with the oil (5/6 g). 3 minutes presentation-side down, 2 minutes over.

  4. Half the glaze spooned over IN the pan on a low heat, tilting and basting for 60 seconds until it goes lacquered.

  5. Broccoli (150/180 g) and carrot (60/70 g) steamed 4 minutes. Cauliflower (100/130 g) stirred through the rice for its last 2 minutes, lid on.

  6. The rest of the glaze over the vegetables. Spring onion (20/25 g) raw over everything.

WhyGlaze at the end, on a low heat. There is sugar in teriyaki and it burns fast — put it in at the start and you get a black pan and a bitter fish long before the middle is warm.
