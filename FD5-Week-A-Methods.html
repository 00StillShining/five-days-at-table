Every gram below is the real portion from the plan, not a rounded-off version of it. Two numbers means her / him — cook both in one pan and split at the plate. Each dish carries one note on the thing that actually decides whether it works.

Meat and fish are raw weights.Grains and pasta are dry weights. Tinned pulses are drained. Everything else is as it comes.
Two covers, one pan.Where a step gives two numbers they are her / him. Cook both together and split at the plate — it is faster and the seasoning is more consistent.
Oil is measured, not poured.It is the single easiest way to add 200 calories to a plate without noticing. A teaspoon is about 4.5 g.
No peas.Anywhere. Where a Caribbean plate would normally call for rice and peas, this one uses red kidney beans and leaves the gungo out.

Bodega egg & cheese

Fresh7 minNew York
Ingredients
IngredientHerHim
Spinach, ugu or callalooRaw100 g120 g
Eggs, medium, shell offShelled100 g100 g2 eggs
Tomatoes, ripeRaw80 g100 g
Tortilla, high-protein low-carbAs it comes45 g50 g1 tortilla
AvocadoFlesh only35 g45 g
Cheddar, reduced fat (~14%)As it comes15 g20 g
Extra virgin olive oilAs it comes5 g8 g2 tsp

  1. Non-stick pan, dry, medium-high. Spinach in (100/120 g) with a pinch of salt. 60 seconds, until it collapses. Tip onto a plate and press the water out with the back of a spoon.

  2. Oil into the pan (5/8 g). Beat the eggs (100 g — 2 eggs) with black pepper. Pour in and tilt to coat the base.

  3. Leave 45 seconds untouched. As the edge sets, draw it to the middle with a spatula and tilt again. Twice more — you want three or four soft folds, not scramble.

  4. Spinach over one half, cheddar on top (15/20 g). Fold, lid on, heat OFF. 60 seconds to melt.

  5. Warm the tortilla (45/50 g) 20 seconds a side in the dry pan while that happens.

  6. Tomato (80/100 g) sliced thin and salted. Avocado (35/45 g) mashed onto the tortilla. Egg in, tomato on, hot sauce, fold and press.

WhyWilt and squeeze. Spinach is mostly water and it will steam the eggs into a wet mess otherwise. And take the pan off the heat before the cheese goes on — residual heat melts it and the egg stays tender.

Chicken-crust pizza

Fresh25 minNaples, reconsidered
Ingredients
IngredientHerHim
Passata, plainAs it comes100 g120 g
Red pepper, deseededPrepared90 g110 g
Chestnut mushroomsRaw90 g110 g
Spinach, ugu or callalooRaw60 g80 g

  1. Oven to 220 °C fan. Tray lined with baking paper.

  2. Base: chicken mince (65/75 g), parmesan (12/15 g), half a beaten egg (25 g), salt, oregano, garlic powder. Mix hard with your hands for a full minute — it should turn tacky and hold together in one mass.

  3. Press onto the paper into a 20 cm round, 5–6 mm thick and even. Wet fingers stop it sticking. Bake 15 minutes.

  4. Meanwhile: pepper (90/110 g), mushrooms (90/110 g) and onion (30/40 g) sliced. Pan, 5 g of the oil, high heat, 5 minutes — until the mushrooms have given up their water and taken colour. Spinach (60/80 g) in at the end to wilt. Season.

  6. Passata (100/120 g) spread thin, mozzarella (30/40 g) torn over, vegetables on. Back in for 8 minutes.

  7. Salad alongside: lettuce (40/50 g), dressed properly with the remaining oil (14/17 g total across the dish), lemon, salt and plenty of black pepper.

WhyBlot the base. Chicken mince releases water as it bakes, and a wet base steams instead of crisping. Press it thin and even too — a thick middle stays raw while the rim burns.
