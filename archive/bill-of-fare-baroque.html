Composed for two covers

Five DaysatTable

Drawn from the carts of New York, the fires of Jamaica, the kitchens of Nigeria & the comfort foods of everywhere — rebuilt lean, and set down for the reduction of fat, the preservation of muscle, and four days of labour under the bar.

Anno MMXXVI

A pizza, a burger, a wrap, a plate of pasta and a breakfast sandwich, alongside efo riro, pepper soup, jerk salmon, a katsu curry and a halal-cart plate — every one of them rebuilt so that it fits the numbers rather than pretending to. The starch is halved throughout: rice cut with cauliflower, a pizza base made of chicken rather than flour, spaghetti pressed from edamame. One Sunday's labour furnishes four luncheons; the rest is cooked fresh and fast. Two sizes of portion are given — select yours below, and every weight and reckoning upon the card will follow.

Part the First

The Sunday Labour

Some fifty-five minutes, the greater part of it spent waiting.

I
Sunday  —  ~55 min, mostly waiting

A tray of chicken breasts seasoned with nothing but salt, garlic and onion powder, into a 200°C oven for twenty-five minutes. This one tray becomes three lunches — the character arrives later, at the plate, as halal-cart spice, as efo riro, as shawarma.

II
Sunday  —  the same oven

Beside it, chickpeas tossed in oil and jerk seasoning, roasted until they rattle. Peppers and onions for the week's bowls on a third tray if there is room.

III
Sunday  —  35 min on the hob

Three pans. The chipotle beef: mince browned hard with chipotle, cumin and oregano. The efo riro base: peppers, tomato and onion blended and cooked down with egusi until the oil separates. The bolognese: turkey mince, passata, grated carrot and courgette, left to mumble for half an hour while you do the rest.

IV
Sunday  —  15 min

The small things that make the week quick. Six eggs boiled eight minutes. Two heads of cauliflower riced in the processor and bagged raw. Cabbage and carrot shredded for the slaws. Garlic yoghurt shaken up in a jar and used three separate times.

V
Wednesday  —  10 min

A short top-up. Fish and prawns bought fresh, more salad, more fruit. Nothing that keeps badly is bought on Sunday.

Remarks from the Kitchen

To be read once, and then not fussed over.

The protein, and the reasoning

A hundred and five to a hundred and sixteen grams on the smaller cover, a hundred and thirty-nine to a hundred and forty-eight on the larger — down some fifty and seventy grams from where this card began, which was frankly more than either body needed. As set, that works out at roughly 1.6 grams per kilogramme of bodyweight for both, which is squarely inside the range that protects muscle while the weight comes off. The figures were scaled to each body rather than fixed at one number: a flat hundred-and-twenty applied to the 85 kg cover would have meant 1.4 grams per kilo, and rather more of the loss arriving from muscle than from fat.

Where the freed calories went

Nowhere, in the sense that matters — the daily totals are unchanged, so the deficit and the rate of loss are exactly as before. What changed is the shape of the plate. Rice portions are larger, plantain is more generous, avocado appears where it did not, the olive oil is no longer measured out meanly, and the fruit is a proper serving rather than a garnish. The food is a good deal less austere than it was.

On the carbohydrate

Lower, not low — the version that survives contact with a real week. No dish is built on a mound of starch: rice is cut half-and-half with riced cauliflower, bread is the seeded low-carb sort, pasta is pressed from edamame, and the pizza has no base at all. Net carbohydrate now lands between ninety-one and a hundred and sixty-one grams on the smaller cover, a hundred and fourteen to two hundred and seventeen on the larger — higher than before, because that is where the protein went.

The fibre

Forty to forty-four grams on the smaller cover, fifty-one to fifty-four on the larger, against a UK recommendation of thirty. Beans, cauliflower, greens and the high-fibre wraps do the work. If it is a sharp jump from where you are now, ease in across the first week and drink considerably more water than feels necessary.

Three things worth buying once

High-fibre low-carb tortillas, edamame or black-bean spaghetti, and a seeded low-carb loaf. These three are what allow a wrap, a plate of pasta and a burger onto a card like this at all; without them those dishes cannot be made to fit, and would have had to be cut.

What is not on the card

A number of dishes were considered and cut because they cannot be made to fit these numbers honestly: pounded yam and fufu, puff-puff, dodo fried properly in oil, a full traditional plate of jollof, moi moi and ewa agoyin (which are built on peas in any case), oxtail and nkwobi, and pizza on real dough. Nothing here is a diet version of itself pretending otherwise — the dishes that survived are the ones that genuinely work at this calorie.

The spice shelf

Nigerian: suya spice, egusi, dried crayfish, scotch bonnet, pepper soup spice, and iru if you can find it. Everything else: jerk seasoning, chipotle in adobo, Japanese curry powder, smoked paprika, dried thyme, oregano. Bought once, they last months, and they are the whole difference between these plates and the same ingredients cooked dully.

How everything is weighed

Meat, fish and poultry are RAW weights, before cooking. Rice, oats and pasta are DRY weights. Tinned pulses are DRAINED weights. Vegetables and fruit are prepared weights — peeled, deseeded, stoned, as the case may be. Where a household measure sits beside the gram figure it is an approximation for the days you cannot be bothered with the scales; the gram figure is what the reckonings are built on.

Peas, swaps & the rest

No peas anywhere — where a Caribbean plate would call for rice and peas this uses red kidney beans, and the Nigerian bean dishes were left off for the same reason. Catfish takes any firm white fish; prawns and chicken trade places freely; skyr and Greek yoghurt are interchangeable.

Calories and reckonings are estimates drawn from standard composition tables and will wander a few parts in the hundred according to brand and cookery. The covers assume ordinary stature and moderate activity away from the bar; after a fortnight, adjust by some hundred and fifty calories in whichever direction the glass and the scale advise. Offered as general counsel on eating, not as medical or dietary prescription.
