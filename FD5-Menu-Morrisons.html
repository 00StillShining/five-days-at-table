The plate side of the Morrisons starter shop — same 10 meals, same reasons the other 5 aren't here. Monday and Tuesday run in full; Wednesday is off completely; Thursday and Friday each keep two of three. Difficulty is tagged on every card — green cooks in under ten minutes with no real technique, orange wants your attention on the pan.

Chicken-crust pizza
Hands-on Italian-American · 25 min ·
The base is the trick: chicken mince pressed thin with parmesan and egg, baked fifteen minutes at 220°C until it firms and browns. Passata over it, mozzarella, mushrooms, peppers, onion and spinach, then back in for eight. It carves like a pizza, lifts like a pizza, and does it without a gram of flour.

Reading the numbers

One line per figure

Kcal, protein, fat and net carb are per portion, already split by the two of you the way the whole plan is — his side runs bigger throughout. Spice is dots out of four, not a warning: the jerk salmon is the only thing here past two. Difficulty is genuinely about attention, not skill — the chicken-crust pizza takes the longest and needs a second bake, everything tagged green is a one-pan job under ten minutes.
