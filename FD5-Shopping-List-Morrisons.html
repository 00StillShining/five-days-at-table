Everything here comes from one Morrisons order — no second shop, no market run. 5 meals are cut, cleanly, because Morrisons doesn't stock what they need: all of Wednesday (egg sauce, efo riro, pepper soup) and Friday's breakfast (callaloo & egg skillet) need plantain — Morrisons' plantain search turns up only plantain crisps. Wednesday's lunch also needs egusi, which returns zero results. Thursday's lunch needs edamame spaghetti and turkey breast mince — neither is stocked (turkey mince here is thigh, not breast). Everything else survives untouched, at real Morrisons prices, verified 11 Aug. This is meant as the on-ramp: fewer meals, one shop, lower total spend, so cooking from the plan is a small commitment rather than a big one.
